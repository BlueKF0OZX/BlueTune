How this check works

BlueTune reads receive-audio statistics reported by ASL3. It checks for clipping and excessive levels. It cannot identify speech, measure RF deviation, or guarantee that audio sounds good.

Live collection supports SimpleUSB only in this preview. Keep other tuning sessions closed while measuring.
